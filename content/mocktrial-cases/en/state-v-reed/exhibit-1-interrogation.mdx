**Recorded interview of Peyton Reed**\
Norwood Police Department — Interview Room 2\
Officer present: Officer Ryan Cole. Miranda warnings administered en route by Officer Norman.

---

**Officer Cole:** We are going on record at 11:32 AM of April 25, 2025 with Peyton Reed.

**Officer Cole:** Alright Mz. Reed, Officer Norman read you your Miranda Rights on the way here. Before I start asking you questions, do you understand those rights?

**Reed:** Yes. I do.

**Officer Cole:** Ok. Mz. Reed, what happened this morning?

**Reed:** Well… I woke up to Jordan screaming. He was on the phone with 911, and asking them to rush to The Ridge as quickly as possible because West… she was *(voice breaking)*… out. I mean out cold. She wouldn't wake up.

**Officer Cole:** Where were you at 11:45 PM last night?

**Reed:** Uh… I don't really remember. Usually that late I'm in bed asleep.

**Officer Cole:** But you weren't last night, were you?

**Reed:** I don't know what you're talking about. If Drew told you I was near "The Family Room" during West's tea time, you should know that he was too.

**Officer Cole:** So you were near "The Family Room" last night?

**Reed:** Oh… um ok I was. But only for a little bit. He was like camped out in that room the whole night.

**Officer Cole:** Mz. Reed, why were you there?

**Officer Cole:** Are you aware of any allergies that Ms. Bailey had?

**Reed:** Uhh I don't know, I mean we've been friends for a long time. We've cooked for each other before so yeah I guess common foods that we've run into I know about. I know she's allergic to salmon and treenuts.

**Officer Cole:** Did you know she was allergic to pineapple extract?

**Reed:** No, I didn't know that.

**Officer Cole:** Showing Mz. Reed exhibit 6. These are Ms. Bailey's medical records. We found them in her closet messed up on the ground. Have you seen these before?

**Reed:** I… uh — no. I haven't.

**Officer Cole:** Mz. Reed, two of your fingerprints were recovered from this piece of paper. So I'm going to ask you again: why were Ms. Bailey's medical records scattered on the ground?

**Reed:** I don't know, I really don't! Maybe Drew got to them. If you should be interviewing anyone it should be him. Everyone in the house knows that he's always held a grudge on West.

**Officer Cole:** Mr. Simon's fingerprints were not found on the medical records. Nobody's was. Except for you. So let's talk about you. Isn't it true Apex holds a group meeting every Sunday?

**Reed:** Yes.

**Officer Cole:** I've read the transcript and your most recent group meeting on April 20 didn't go so well, did it?

**Reed:** Yeah it went beyond terrible. Our channel's growth is slowing. And better yet, we all wanted to take Apex in different directions. I mean we dropped out of school three months before graduation to pursue this content thing. We all have a lot on the line.

**Officer Cole:** You and Ms. Bailey have the highest decision-making power in the channel, right?

**Reed:** Yes, we both own 30% of Apex. Now tell me how that makes any sense… West has done nothing… absolutely NOTHING for this channel since day 1. She wasn't even here on day 1. Only I was. I came up with this idea, I started this channel. I don't know how we even ended up in this world where we're equals!

**Officer Cole:** You wanted Ms. Bailey out of the group meeting next Sunday, didn't you?

**Reed:** Yes. I wanted her gone. I wanted her out of this channel and out of my life forever.

**Officer Cole:** That's why you bought the pineapple extract?

**Reed:** What? No. I had nothing to do with that pineapple extract.

**Officer Cole:** You're the one who bought it, didn't you?

**Reed:** I — uh… I don't want to answer that question.

**Officer Cole:** Ok Mz. Reed, thank you for speaking with me today. How about we regroup and the station will call you back in for a follow-up tomorrow.
